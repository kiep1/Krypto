A TRANSPARENT PROCESS

How AI chart analysis works

The analyzer starts with a chart screenshot and returns a structured interpretation. Each result should be checked against the image and treated as a learning aid.

1. Choose a chart image

Upload a PNG, JPG, or WEBP screenshot up to 8 MB. A useful image makes the visible price scale, timeframe, and relevant chart area easy to read. Crop out unrelated information where practical.

Do not include passwords, private keys, account numbers, or other sensitive information. The image is sent to OpenAI to generate the response. If you are signed in, you can choose to save the finished chart and report to your private journal; it is not saved there automatically.

2. Add context

You can enter an instrument or market, select a timeframe, and ask a question. These fields help frame the explanation, but they do not give the analyzer access to current prices or data outside the screenshot.

If the chart is missing a readable scale or the question depends on information that is not shown, the report should say so instead of filling in the gaps.

3. Review the report

The report separates a possible scenario from the evidence behind it. It may describe visible structure, possible support or resistance areas, an invalidation condition, and levels only when those levels can be read reliably.

Read the limitations alongside the scenario. A clearly worded explanation can still be wrong. The analyzer does not place orders, connect to a brokerage account, or monitor a chart after the request ends.

What the score means

The 0–100 score is an assessment of the quality and completeness of visible evidence. It is not a forecast, win rate, confidence that a trade will be profitable, or a probability of a market move.

The separate risk label describes uncertainty in interpreting the image. It is not a calculation of the visitor’s personal or portfolio risk.

Limits and data handling

The tool only analyzes the information visible in the image and context you provide. It does not fetch live market data or independently verify a chart provider’s data. AI may misread labels, lines, or candle details.

Images are sent to OpenAI to produce a report. A report is added to your journal only if you are signed in and choose “Save to my journal.” You can delete saved reports from your journal. Avoid uploading screenshots that contain private details. Read how account data is handled.
