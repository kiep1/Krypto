CandleTutor frequently asked questions

Straight answers about chart analysis, account access, daily limits, saved charts, and Premium.

What does a score such as 85/100 mean?

It rates the quality and completeness of evidence visible in the image. It is not an 85% chance of profit, a win rate, or a prediction.

Does CandleTutor use live market prices?

No. The analyzer reviews the image and context you provide. It does not fetch live prices or verify whether a chart remains current.

Is a BUY or SELL result a recommendation?

No. Those labels describe possible educational scenarios. They are not instructions to open, close, or change a position. WAIT can mean the image does not support a clear interpretation.

What happens to an uploaded image?

The image and context you provide are sent to OpenAI to generate the analysis. They are not automatically saved to your account. If you are signed in, you can choose “Save to my journal” after the report appears. You can delete a saved record from your journal. Do not upload passwords, private keys, account numbers, or other sensitive information. See the privacy and data page.

What does Premium include and how does billing work?

Premium includes eight advanced lessons, up to 30 chart analyses per UTC day, and up to 50 lesson-tutor answers per UTC day. You can start with a 7-day free trial; a payment method is required. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, Stripe charges $9.99 USD and renews the subscription monthly until cancelled. Cancel in your account before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. Checkout shows the trial and recurring price before confirmation.

Can the AI misread my chart?

Yes. It may misread labels, scales, lines, candles, or the timeframe, and a screenshot may not contain enough information. Check the explanation against the image and consider outside context.

Does the tool execute trades or connect to an exchange?

No. It does not place orders or connect to a brokerage or exchange account.

What file types can I upload?

The analyzer accepts PNG, JPG, and WEBP images up to 8 MB.
