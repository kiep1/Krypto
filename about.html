PURPOSE & EDITORIAL APPROACH

About CandleTutor

CandleTutor is an educational tool for thinking through what a chart image shows, what it might suggest, and what cannot be known from the image alone.

Our purpose

CandleTutor helps people practise reading price charts and asking better questions about evidence, uncertainty, and risk. The analyzer turns a submitted screenshot into a structured educational explanation. The learning library explains chart-reading concepts in plain language.

The tool is not a broker, exchange, trading service, or investment adviser. It does not execute trades or make decisions for visitors.

How we approach analysis

A chart image is incomplete evidence. Labels, scales, indicators, and timeframes may be missing or difficult to read. For that reason, the analyzer is designed to distinguish visible observations from possible interpretations.

  • It should not invent prices, indicators, timeframes, or patterns that are not visible.
  • A score describes the completeness and clarity of the evidence in the image. It is not a probability of profit.
  • BUY and SELL are possible educational scenarios, not instructions to trade.
  • WAIT can be the most useful answer when the image does not support a clear interpretation.

What we do not claim

CandleTutor does not use live market prices in its image analysis. It cannot confirm whether an apparent chart level is current, and it cannot know a visitor’s financial circumstances, objectives, or tolerance for loss. An AI-generated interpretation can be incomplete or wrong.

Use the tool to learn, not to make a financial decision for you. Verify anything important with reliable, current sources and consider qualified independent advice where appropriate.

How our content is produced

Learning pages are written with AI assistance and reviewed for clarity and consistency. They are educational explainers, not personalized recommendations. We do not claim that they have been reviewed by a licensed financial professional.

We aim to explain both the usefulness and the limits of chart-based interpretation. We avoid profit promises, fabricated performance claims, invented author credentials, and language that treats a pattern as a guaranteed signal.
