Eight advanced lessons

Premium unlocks lessons on support and resistance zones, order execution, technical indicators, position sizing, backtesting, journaling, crypto market risks, and trading psychology. The first three course lessons remain free.

More chart practice

Free accounts receive three chart analyses per UTC day. Premium includes up to 30 per UTC day. After any analysis, signed-in users can choose whether to save its chart image and report in their private journal.

A tutor for lesson questions

Ask about the concepts in the lesson you are studying. The tutor is limited to educational explanations and does not provide a personalized trade recommendation. Free accounts can ask five lesson questions per UTC day; Premium includes up to 50.

Trading involves risk. The course and AI analysis do not provide financial advice, live market data, or guaranteed outcomes.

Access, billing, and cancellation

Premium features are unlocked after a verified Stripe subscription event reaches the application. The seven-day trial begins when you complete checkout. Cancel from your account before the trial ends to avoid the first $9.99 USD monthly charge. If you do not cancel, the subscription renews automatically each month until cancelled. Manage or cancel through Stripe billing management from your account page. Account creation, checkout, and membership require the secure sign-in and database setup to be active.
