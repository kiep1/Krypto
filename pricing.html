Daily limits

Usage limits reset at 00:00 UTC. Free includes three AI chart analyses and five lesson-tutor answers per day. Premium includes up to 30 analyses and 50 lesson-tutor answers per day. Premium limits are intentionally capped to keep the AI service predictable.

Billing and cancellation

Premium starts with a 7-day free trial. You must provide a payment method to start it, but the subscription price is $0 during those seven days. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, Stripe will automatically charge $9.99 USD and renew the subscription every month until you cancel. The Stripe checkout page shows the trial, first payment date, and recurring price before you confirm. To avoid the first charge, sign in and cancel from your account before the trial ends. After a paid renewal, cancellation stops future renewals; Premium access remains until the current paid period ends.

Sign-in, checkout, and saved journals depend on the site’s secure database and account configuration. If the account page says setup is in progress, membership checkout is not available yet; no payment should be made until the account page offers the Stripe upgrade flow.
