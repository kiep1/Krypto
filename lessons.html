STRUCTURED LEARNING PATH

Trading lessons for careful chart readers

Build a repeatable process for reading price charts, comparing interpretations, and understanding uncertainty. The first three lessons are free; the complete course is part of Premium.

What you will learn

The CandleTutor course is an 11-lesson introduction to reading trading charts with care. Begin with chart basics and candlesticks, then study market structure, support and resistance zones, order types, and indicators in context. Later lessons introduce risk and position sizing, backtesting, journaling, crypto market risks, and trading psychology. The first three lessons are available free, so you can see the teaching style before choosing whether Premium is right for you.

Each lesson focuses on a process for examining evidence rather than a guaranteed setup. Learn to describe what is visible, consider more than one possible interpretation, identify what would weaken an idea, and keep uncertainty in view. Examples are educational and do not provide live prices, personalized investment advice, or instructions to place a trade. Take your time, compare the material with the chart, and use a paper-trading journal if you want to practice without risking money.
